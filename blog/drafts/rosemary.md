# Rosemary

*Original post from April 13, 2023*

---

Rosemary's traditional and modern applications in wellness. The herb thrives in dry conditions and produces a distinctive fragrance from its needle-shaped leaves.

## Traditional Uses

Western herbalists classify rosemary as warming, with beneficial effects on circulation and digestion. The plant supports liver and gallbladder function, potentially aids hair health, and may serve as a natural alertness booster.

## Research Findings

Thai researchers documented that inhaling rosemary essential oil increased heart rate, breathing, and blood pressure while enhancing feelings of freshness and activity levels. The compound works on the sympathetic nervous system, promoting blood flow and pain relief while supporting digestive comfort.

## Emerging Research

Scientists have identified compounds—carnosol, carnosic acid, and rosmanol—showing potential anticancer activity through immune system support, inflammatory reduction, and programmed cell death promotion.

## Culinary Application

The herb is recognized as safe for food use and can enhance meats, legumes, stews, and dips with both flavor and nutritional benefit.
