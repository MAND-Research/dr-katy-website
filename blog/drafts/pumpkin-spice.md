# Pumpkin Spice: A Brilliant Herbal Remedy Hiding in Plain Sight

*Original post from April 13, 2023*

---

Pumpkin spice may appear trendy, but from an herbal medicine standpoint, this combination offers genuine therapeutic value. The blend supports digestive health and helps prevent upper respiratory infections, particularly during cold months when it's traditionally consumed.

The four primary herbs in pumpkin spice each contribute specific medicinal benefits:

**Cinnamon** helps regulate blood sugar in diabetics and reduces insulin resistance. Herbally, it provides warming properties, induces light perspiration to reduce fever, and promotes healthy digestion.

**Ginger** functions as a warming digestive tonic, addressing nausea, vomiting, burping, flatulence, and cramping. It improves circulation, enhances blood lipids, lowers blood sugar, and loosens lung congestion associated with colds.

**Nutmeg** addresses digestive complaints including nausea, indigestion, gas, poor appetite, diarrhea, and cramping. It also calms the nervous system, supports sleep quality, and helps relieve nasal congestion and sinus issues.

**Clove** promotes blood circulation in the stomach and lungs, addressing upper respiratory infections, cough, asthma, nausea, indigestion, and gas.

Together, these herbs create a warming formula that supports respiratory and digestive health during cold season. However, dosage matters—excessive consumption could prove counterproductive. Consult a practitioner before significantly increasing intake to address symptoms.

## Recipe: Knock-Your-Socks-Off Pumpkin Spice Tea

**Ingredients:**
- 4 cups boiling water
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- Thumb-sized chunk fresh ginger, sliced into ¼-inch pieces
- 6 cloves
- ¼ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg

**Instructions:**

Combine the first three ingredients in boiling water and simmer 15 minutes. Add nutmeg, stir, simmer briefly, then strain and serve immediately.
