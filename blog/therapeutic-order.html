Therapeutic Order: What It Is, Why You Should Care

The therapeutic order categorizes healing approaches from least invasive to most invasive. Lower-risk interventions require more time, while faster treatments carry greater risks and potential side effects.

The Seven Categories

  1. Lifestyle foundations (diet, exercise, stress management)
  2. Body's innate healing support (homeopathy, reiki, acupuncture)
  3. Structural correction (chiropractic, massage)
  4. Natural targeted therapies (herbal medicine)
  5. Supplemental therapies (nutritional supplements)
  6. Pharmaceutical medications
  7. Surgery

Why This Matters

More severe conditions need a higher level of intervention, with surgery and pharmaceuticals being essential for serious cases. However, starting with lower-level approaches often produces superior outcomes.

The Foundation Analogy

Think of two similar houses facing a storm. The house with a strong foundation sustains minimal damage, while the weaker one requires extensive repairs. Similarly, with a strong foundation, you'll be able to better withstand illness and recover faster.

The Key Benefit

Bodies are great at healing themselves. Using this approach allows the body to do most of the healing work, yielding lasting results with fewer side effects.
