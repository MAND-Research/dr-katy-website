## The Three Phases

**Headline:** How we work together

### Phase One: Gather

We start by slowing down and listening. Your story, your symptoms, your life. I'll review your health history for patterns conventional care often misses and run labs that look beyond "normal ranges" to what your body is doing. By the end, you'll have clarity and a plan built for your real life.

### Phase Two: Align

Now we implement. Acupuncture, herbs, supplements, sometimes medication—whatever fits best, chosen together. We track how your body responds and adjust as we go.

### Phase Three: Flourish

As your body finds balance, the practices supporting you become part of your natural rhythm. You'll have the knowledge to maintain your progress and confidence to trust what you're noticing. We'll make a plan for what comes next.

## Common Questions

**How do I become a patient?**

The first step is a free 15-minute consultation. We'll talk through what's going on and see if we're a good fit. [→ More on the new patient process]

**Do I have to sign up for the whole program?**

Yes. Elemental Health is designed as a complete program because meaningful health changes take consistent care over time. After the initial four months, you can continue with follow-ups or transition to Elemental Care based on what makes sense for you.

**I've tried so many things that haven't worked. How is this different?**

Most approaches either throw everything at you at once or focus on one thing in isolation. We identify what matters most right now and start there. You'll understand why we're doing what we're doing, which makes it easier to stick with.

**What if I don't have time for big lifestyle changes?**

That's exactly why we find your starting place instead of handing you a 20-item protocol. Sometimes the highest-impact intervention is the simplest one. We work with your current capacity.

**What if my labs come back "normal" but I still feel terrible?**

"Normal" ranges are wide. You might feel terrible at one end and great at the other. I look for optimal, not just normal. And Chinese medicine shows me patterns labs can't measure. You won't be dismissed here.

**What's included in the lab panel?**

CBC, CMP, Lipid Panel, TSH, free T4, free T3, Vitamin B12/Folate, Vitamin D, Iron/TIBC, and Ferritin. Phlebotomy included. If additional testing makes sense, we'll discuss options and costs before ordering. [→ More on lab testing]

**Do you take insurance?**

I don't bill insurance directly. If your plan covers out-of-network naturopathic medicine or acupuncture, I can provide a superbill for reimbursement. [→ More on insurance and billing]

**How long before I'll feel better?**

It varies. Some people notice shifts in sleep, energy, or mood within weeks. Others see more gradual changes. We track your progress and adjust as we go.

**Do I need to come to Rockland?**

Not for everything. Acupuncture requires in-person visits, but follow-ups and lab reviews can be virtual. Many patients mix both based on what works for them.

**Can you be my primary care provider?**

I provide integrative care meant to complement—not replace—primary care. I encourage all patients to maintain a relationship with a PCP for routine checkups and urgent concerns. [→ More on this]

**What do you treat?**

Hormonal health (fertility, perimenopause, menopause, thyroid, PCOS, cycle issues), digestive issues (IBS, GERD, bloating), and mental health concerns (anxiety, depression, insomnia, burnout). If you're not sure whether I can help with your situation, that's what the consultation is for. [→ More on what I treat]
