Lab testing
What your labs aren't telling you
1
Reference ranges aren't optimal ranges
Standard lab ranges include the middle 95% of people tested, including those who are unwell.
2
Context changes everything
The same result can mean different things depending on your symptoms, history, and other markers.
3
Trends matter more than snapshots
A single test is a moment in time. Patterns across tests reveal the real story.
4
Normal doesn't mean nothing is wrong
"Normal labs" with persistent symptoms is a signal, not a dismissal.
