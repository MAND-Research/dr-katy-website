## Common Questions

**Do I have to use all 8 visits?**

No. Some patients come monthly during demanding seasons and less often when things are stable. The visits flex to what you need.

**What if I need more than 8 visits?**

You can add visits at $250 each while keeping all your membership benefits.

**Can I use visits for acupuncture, naturopathic care, or both?**

Yes. Each visit can be naturopathic consultation, acupuncture, or a combined session. We choose what's most helpful for where you are.

**Can I adjust how often I come throughout the year?**

Yes. Some phases need more frequent support; others are about maintaining progress. Schedule visits closer together during intensive periods or space them out when things are stable.

**How does the annual lab panel work?**

We schedule your comprehensive panel about 12 months from your previous labs. If something needs monitoring more frequently, we order targeted panels as needed.

**Does Elemental Care auto-renew?**

No. At the end of your 12 months, we'll check in about whether continuing makes sense for where you are. Re-enrollment is always your choice.

**What happens if I don't renew?**

Your package benefits (messaging, labs, discounts) end on your package end date. You have 6 months to use any remaining visits. After that, you can schedule single visits anytime.

**Can I pay in full instead of monthly?**

Yes. Contact the office to arrange pay-in-full.

**Do you take insurance?**

I don't bill insurance directly. If your plan covers out-of-network naturopathic medicine or acupuncture, I can provide a superbill for reimbursement. Superbills cover visit fees only (not labs or administrative fees).
