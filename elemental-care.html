Work With Us

For Established Patients

Elemental Care

Ongoing care with a doctor who already knows your story.

See what's included

You've already done the deeper work with me. You understand your body's patterns. Now you need consistent support, not a series of disconnected appointments.

Elemental Care is how we continue: regular access to a doctor who knows your history, your patterns, and how your body responds. When something shifts, you don't start over. You pick up where we left off.

Why ongoing care

You have a doctor, not just appointments

Someone who knows your full picture and can respond when something comes up. No re-explaining your history to someone new.

Proactive beats reactive

Regular touchpoints catch patterns early: the thyroid shift, the cortisol creep, the nutrient depletion. Before they become symptoms you have to dig out of.

Less friction

A question comes up, you message me. A symptom shifts, we adjust. No gatekeeping, no hunting for availability.

Elemental Care

What's included over 12 months

  • Up to 8 visits: Scheduled when you need them, not on an arbitrary timeline
  • Flexible visit types: Naturopathic consultations, acupuncture, or both
  • Direct messaging access: Reach me between visits for questions and support
  • Annual comprehensive lab panel: With written summary to track your progress
  • 15% off supplements: Professional-grade supplements recommended for your care
  • Administrative support: Paperwork, referrals, and prior authorizations handled

Additional visits available at $250 each. HSA/FSA eligible.

Very. You don't have to use all 8 visits, and you can adjust frequency throughout the year based on what you need. Some patients come monthly during demanding seasons and space visits out when things are stable. If you need more than 8, you can add visits at $250 each.

We schedule your comprehensive panel about 12 months from your previous labs. If something needs monitoring more frequently, we order targeted panels as needed.

Elemental Care doesn't auto-renew. At the end of your 12 months, we'll check in about whether continuing makes sense. If you don't renew, your benefits (messaging, labs, discounts) end on your package date, but you have 6 months to use any remaining visits.

Monthly payments of $250 are standard. Pay-in-full is also available (contact the office to arrange). I don't bill insurance directly, but if your plan covers out-of-network naturopathic medicine or acupuncture, I can provide a superbill for reimbursement.

Single visits are available at $325 for patients who prefer occasional check-ins without an ongoing commitment. These visits don't include messaging access, labs, supplement discounts, or administrative support. More on visit options →
