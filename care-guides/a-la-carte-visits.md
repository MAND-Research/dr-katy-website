# Understanding À La Carte Visits

À la carte visits provide focused care when you need it, without requiring a long-term program commitment.

## What's Included

- Focused time with Dr. Katy
- Physical examination when needed
- Acupuncture, if desired
- Treatment planning
- Review of labs or documents you bring
- Clear next steps for your care

## Communication

- Our admin team is available for scheduling and logistics
- All clinical discussions happen during your visits
- Between-visit messaging with Dr. Katy is reserved for care program members

## Additional Services

The following require additional fees or a care program:

- Lab ordering and processing
- Administrative services (referrals, forms)
- Supplement discounts

## Preparing for Your Visit

- Bring relevant health records
- List current supplements and medications
- Note your main concerns
- Plan to schedule follow-up visits as needed

Looking for more comprehensive support? Learn about the clinic's care programs through our Elemental Care offerings.
