# Between Visits: Messaging Guidelines for Ongoing Care

Midcoast Health Collective has established communication guidelines to support patient care while maintaining sustainable healthcare practices. Meaningful healing occurs within dedicated appointment spaces, with messaging serving a supplementary role.

## Appropriate Uses for Messaging

The practice accepts messages for:

- Appointment confirmation or supplement pickup scheduling
- Document sharing for review at upcoming visits
- Brief updates related to existing care plans
- Notifications about illness requiring rescheduling

## When to Schedule a Visit Instead

Please book an appointment rather than message for:

- New or changing symptoms
- Medication or supplement modifications
- Lab result interpretation
- Questions requiring clinical decision-making

A helpful question to ask yourself: Would this conversation benefit from the unhurried space of a visit, rather than the back-and-forth of messaging?

## Response Timeline

Non-urgent messages receive replies within 2-3 business days. Care plan patients experiencing urgent clinical concerns like treatment reactions receive faster responses. The messaging system is not monitored continuously and cannot address emergencies—if you're experiencing a medical emergency, please call 911.

## Access by Patient Type

**Care Plan Patients:** Included structured between-visit messaging support for ongoing care management, though complex clinical issues may require scheduled visits.

**À La Carte Patients:** Between-visit clinical messaging is unavailable; scheduling a visit ensures concerns receive proper attention.

This approach prioritizes intentional, focused care while supporting sustainable clinic operations.
