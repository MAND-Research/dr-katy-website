# Elemental Care Program Timeline and Renewal

## During Your Program Year

- Your program benefits run for 12 months from enrollment
- Benefits include messaging access, labs, and supplement discounts
- Schedule visits at intervals that work for you

## Renewal Process

- We'll reach out about a month before your program's end date
- Review your care experience and goals for the coming year
- Choose to continue your current program or adjust your level of care
- Complete quick enrollment with no interruption in benefits
- Feel free to let us know your renewal decision any time

## If You Don't Renew

- Program benefits (messaging, labs, discounts) end on your program's end date
- You have 6 months to use any remaining visits
- Schedule à la carte visits anytime after your program ends
