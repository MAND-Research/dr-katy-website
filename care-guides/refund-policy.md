# Refund Policy

## Supplements

You can return unopened, unexpired supplements within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. However, opened or temperature-sensitive products cannot be returned due to safety and storage concerns.

## Lab Fees

Laboratory charges are processed after sample collection through either a draw site or home kit. Once processed, these fees are considered final and cannot be refunded, even if you discontinue care or decline to review results.

## Services

Services that have already been provided are non-refundable.

## Care Programs

Prepaid care programs (such as Elemental Health or Elemental Care) may qualify for partial refunds if terminated early. An early cancellation fee of 15% applies, plus deductions for services already rendered. These programs require commitment and we recommend enrolling only when ready for full engagement. In exceptional circumstances involving serious illness or major life disruption, we may consider pausing or modifying a program on a case-by-case basis, though this is not guaranteed.

## Important Note

This policy is current but may change. For specific details, please consult your intake documents or contact the clinic directly.
