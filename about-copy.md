# About Dr. Katy - Copy

## Opening

I'm a naturopathic doctor and acupuncturist specializing in midlife women's health. I practice in Rockland, Maine.

I take my time with patients. I ask a lot of questions, I run the labs that make sense, and I build plans around what's actually going on rather than a template. That's the kind of care I wanted when I was struggling with my own health, and it's what I offer here.

---

## My Approach

I trained in both naturopathic medicine and Chinese medicine, and I use them together.

The naturopathic lens gives me lab work, biochemistry, and evidence-based interventions. I can order tests, interpret results, and prescribe treatments (including some medications when they make sense). The Chinese medicine lens shows me patterns that labs can't measure. It helps me understand why your energy crashes at 3pm, or why your digestion is fine until you're stressed, or why one symptom always seems to trigger another.

One framework asks what's happening in your body. The other asks why. I find the overlap is where the answers usually are.

This means I won't dismiss you because your labs came back normal. And I won't hand you a generic protocol. We figure out what's going on and what to do about it, together.

---

## Why This Work

My own experience with PCOS and infertility taught me what it feels like to want real answers and not get them. To be told everything looks fine when you know something is off. That experience shaped how I practice.

I don't rush. I don't minimize. And I don't assume we already know the answer before we've looked.

---

## Background

I came to medicine through an unusual path. I studied biology and ethnobotany (how cultures use plants as medicine), then worked in research before realizing I wanted to work directly with people. I grew up in Maine and came back to practice here. My husband runs the office; our son and dog keep us busy outside of it.
