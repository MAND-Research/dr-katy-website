# Commitment Agreement
## Elemental Health & Elemental Care

**Let's stay committed to your health.**

This is an agreement between you and Dr. Katy Morrison of Midcoast Health Collective for your participation in either the Elemental Health or Elemental Care programs.

Whether you're just beginning care or continuing with ongoing support, this agreement helps clarify expectations and shared commitments. We are dedicated to providing you with skilled, thoughtful care and personalized guidance, and we ask that you engage with openness, consistency, and a willingness to follow your care plan.

Please review the details below, including program benefits, telemedicine consent, and privacy policies.

Clear expectations on both sides help us do our best work together.

---

## Program Benefits

The following services are included in both the Elemental Health and Elemental Care programs:

**Annual Lab Panel:** One comprehensive lab panel is included per year, covering:
- CBC with differential, CMP, Lipid Panel, TSH, Free T4, Free T3, Vitamin B12/Folate, Vitamin D, Iron/TIBC, Ferritin.

**Supplement Discount:** Ongoing 15% discount on professional-grade supplements ordered through our clinic.

**Administrative Services:** Administrative tasks such as referral letters, prior authorizations, and medical forms (e.g., FMLA, school/work notes) are included at no additional cost during your package period.

**Secure Messaging Access:** Non-urgent questions and updates may be submitted through our HIPAA-compliant messaging platform. Messages are responded to during business hours (Monday-Thursday). Messaging is not appropriate for urgent or emergency care. [Read more about our messaging guidelines here.]

**Specialist Coordination:** We will coordinate with any medical specialists you are referred to. Program fees do not cover any outside providers or services beyond the clinic.

**Flexible Additional Visits:** Additional visits available at a discounted rate, with retained access to the above benefits.

---

## Elemental Care Package Term & Re-enrollment

Your Elemental Care package is valid for 12 months from the date of your first visit within the plan. This package does not auto-renew.

As your package nears its end, you will receive a reminder with the option to re-enroll. If you choose not to re-enroll, you may continue care on an à la carte basis, understanding that services such as messaging, labs, and administrative support are not included outside of scheduled visits.

Re-enrollment is subject to availability and current pricing at the time of renewal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Canceling the Agreement Before the End of the Contracted Period:** If this Agreement is canceled by either party before the package term ends, Midcoast Health Collective will refund a prorated share of the payment within 30 days, minus a 15% early cancellation fee and the cost of any services already rendered.

**Insurance or Other Medical Coverage:** This agreement is not a substitute for insurance. It does not cover emergency care, hospital services, or services provided by other healthcare professionals. You are encouraged to maintain separate health insurance and a primary care provider.

**Medical Services:** Your care plan may include diagnostic work-up, treatments, or referrals based on your individual needs. Any additional services not included in your package will be discussed in advance and are optional.
