The pattern

You've probably felt like you needed to fix everything at once. Overhaul your diet, start a supplement protocol, exercise more, sleep better, stress less.

But you're already stretched thin. And when you can't maintain the morning routine you know would help, it starts to feel like a willpower problem.

It's not.

The shift

You don't need to do more. You need to know where to begin.

Fatigue, digestive issues, sleep problems, anxiety. Sometimes the cause is obvious once someone looks closely. Sometimes it takes digging into your history, running the right labs, and understanding how your systems connect. Either way, symptoms aren't random. They're information.

Once you have that information, the question becomes: where do you start?

That's what Elemental Health helps you figure out.

The work

Elemental Health is a four-month program that starts with understanding your whole picture, then focuses on the one or two systems that need attention first.

Not more interventions. The right ones, in the right order, for your life as it is.

You'll work with me through three phases: gathering information, implementing your plan, and building practices that stick. The goal isn't just feeling better. It's feeling better and knowing how to stay there.

How we work together

1

Gather

We start by slowing down and listening. Your story, your symptoms, your life. I'll review your health history for patterns conventional care often misses and run labs that look beyond "normal ranges" to what your body is doing. By the end, you'll have clarity and a plan built for your real life.

2

Align

Now we implement. Acupuncture, herbs, supplements, sometimes medication (whatever fits best, chosen together). We track how your body responds and adjust as we go.

3

Flourish

As your body finds balance, the practices supporting you become part of your natural rhythm. You'll have the knowledge to maintain your progress and confidence to trust what you're noticing. We'll make a plan for what comes next.

My Approach

Two lenses, one picture

I'm a licensed naturopathic doctor and acupuncturist. This dual training matters because I'm looking at your health through two complementary frameworks at once.

The naturopathic lens gives me lab work, biochemistry, and evidence-based interventions. Chinese medicine shows me patterns and connections that labs can't measure. One issue, multiple ways in.

You won't be dismissed because your labs look "normal." You won't get a template protocol. And you won't have to choose between modern medicine and traditional wisdom.

What comes next

After Elemental Health

At the end of four months, we'll assess where you are and what makes sense going forward.
