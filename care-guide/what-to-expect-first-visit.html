Getting Started

What to Expect at Your First Visit

First Visit at a Glance

  • Visit length: 90 minutes (virtual or in-person)
  • Complete intake forms at least 72 hours before your appointment
  • Labs are ordered after the first visit — we'll talk through what's most useful once we have your full health context
  • Acupuncture is included at your first visit (if you'd like) — wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Use your Practice Better portal to:
    • Fill out forms
    • Schedule follow-ups
    • Upload past labs or documents you'd like me to see
  • Care plan delivered within a few days
  • Messaging access begins once you enroll in the Elemental Health program
  • 24+ hour notice required for cancellations (exceptions for illness or emergencies)

What to Expect

Starting something new—especially when it comes to your health—can stir up a mix of relief, hope, hesitation, or even fatigue. The goal is to meet you exactly where you are and help you feel held, not hurried. This first visit is where that begins.

Whether meeting virtually or in the Rockland office, your initial 90-minute session gives time to understand your story, explore what's been working (and what hasn't), and begin to sketch a path forward.

Before We Meet

Once your first visit is confirmed, you'll receive access to the patient portal via Practice Better. The intake forms ask about your health history, current symptoms, daily rhythms, and priorities. The more you share ahead of time, the more deeply we can focus during your visit.

You can also upload any past lab results or documents for review. New labs won't be ordered before meeting—if relevant, that discussion happens at your first appointment.

The Visit Itself

This appointment is not rushed. You'll have time to tell your story fully—medical details and the parts often missed: what your body communicates, what you sense beneath the surface, and what you long for in this health season.

The visit covers how naturopathic and Chinese medicine work together in your care, what tools might be used, and what pace feels sustainable. There's space to ask questions and make decisions together.

Afterward

Within a few days, you'll receive a care plan with recommendations and a suggested timeline for follow-up visits. Your plan will continue shaping together as you go.

Once enrolled in the Elemental Health program, you'll have access to secure messaging through Practice Better for brief check-ins or clarifications between visits. This isn't designed for urgent care but helps maintain connection.
