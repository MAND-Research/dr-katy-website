Our Approach to Lab Testing

Laboratory testing at Midcoast Health Collective serves as a key diagnostic tool that supports personalized, whole-person care. However, the clinic maintains a specific policy: tests are only ordered during or after scheduled appointments, not as standalone services.

Rationale for Appointment-Based Testing

Ordering labs outside clinical visits frequently results in suboptimal outcomes. Testing decisions made without current context may lead to incorrect test selection, missed follow-up opportunities, or unnecessary repeat testing. By anchoring lab orders to actual appointments, we ensure testing remains clinically relevant, cost-effective, and actionable within an established care plan.

Standard Lab Process

When testing is recommended, you'll receive a personalized testing plan developed during your visit, along with preparation instructions such as fasting requirements. Results are typically reviewed at a subsequent appointment according to our Lab Results Communication Policy.

Elemental Care Program Exception

Patients enrolled in the Elemental Care package receive an annual lab panel as part of their services. However, the timing and scope of that panel are still determined during early visits based on clinical need and prior lab history.

Common Questions

Can I get labs ordered before my first visit?

Labs cannot be ordered before the first visit due to the legal requirement of establishing a doctor-patient relationship.

What about repeat testing for established patients?

Repeat testing only occurs if previously planned within your treatment protocol.

Can you order labs based on another provider's recommendation?

A full visit is required to evaluate recommendations from other healthcare practitioners.

When will I get my results?

Lab result interpretation happens during scheduled appointments unless already part of a documented care plan.
