Understanding Your Lab Results

How we communicate and interpret your lab results.

At Midcoast Health Collective, lab results are interpreted within the context of your complete health picture. Numbers alone rarely tell the complete story of what's happening in your body.

Standard Process

When ordering tests, we carefully select assessments relevant to your situation. Results typically arrive within 3-7 business days, after which the office reviews findings for urgent concerns. We discuss results during scheduled follow-up appointments rather than immediately, allowing us to interpret findings alongside symptoms and medical history while answering questions comprehensively.

Rationale for Delayed Discussion

Lab values require clinical context. Results appearing abnormal might actually be normal for you, while seemingly normal findings could indicate areas needing support when considered with other health factors.

Special Circumstances

Urgent situations

We contact you immediately if results indicate serious concerns.

Early access requests

You may request results through the patient portal before appointments, receiving them within 5 business days. However, we discourage this approach and messaging-based interpretation won't be available.

Post-appointment access

After appointments, you'll receive printed results and portal access for your records.
