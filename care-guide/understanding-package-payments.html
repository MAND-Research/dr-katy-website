Billing

Understanding Program Payments

Clear, consistent payment processing helps you focus on what matters—your health.

Payment Plans

  • Choose between monthly payments or pay-in-full at enrollment
  • Pay-in-full option includes a discount
  • Monthly payment plans can be paid off early if desired
  • Early payoff doesn't qualify for the pay-in-full discount
  • Early cancellation available only per the cancellation policy

Monthly Payment Schedule

  • Payments process automatically on your enrollment date each month
  • If you enrolled on the 31st, payments process on the last day of each month
  • Need to adjust your payment date? Contact the office

Insurance and HSA Documentation

  • Superbills provided for completed visits upon request
  • Documentation available for HSA/FSA reimbursement
  • Superbills cover visit fees only (not labs or administrative fees)
  • Request documentation through the office

Questions about payments? Contact the office.
